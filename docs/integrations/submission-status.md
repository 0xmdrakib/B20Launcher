## Submission details

GeckoTerminal and Uniswap received their [prepared technical requests](onboarding-requests.md). Uniswap's form category was **Uniswap Web App**, issue **Explore page**, network **Base**. Its required wallet and transaction fields identify the representative public test launch; the request explicitly concerns metadata ingestion.

MetaMask's **Contact Support** route opened its live support messenger. The inquiry was sent there, then routed to the human support queue under **Extension**, with the feed, documentation and technical context. The follow-up email was supplied in its required field and the ticket detail confirmed submission. An answer from the separate AI search page is not recorded as integration acceptance.

DEX Screener's official Discord FAQ provides a partnership form with **Integrations** as an area of interest. The prepared DEX Screener request was submitted using that category and the owner's supplied contact/profile details. The form confirmed delivery. No DEX-listing claim or paid individual profile order was made.
